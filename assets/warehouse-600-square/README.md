# Warehouse 600 × 600 — Square-corner

These images are the square-corner group within the Antique Screen-printing Craft series.

## Specifications for this folder only

- Size: 600 × 600 mm
- Packing: 4 pieces / carton
- Gross weight: 31 kg / carton

These specifications apply **only** to the images in `warehouse-600-square`. They are not universal specifications for the Antique Screen-printing Craft series or for other sizes in that series.
